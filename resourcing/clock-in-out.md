# Clock In / Clock Out

Accurate time tracking is essential for both employee payroll and precise job costing. PrairieHQ supports mobile time tracking with location verification.

## Time Tracking Modes

1. **Individual Mobile Clock-In**:
   - Technicians log into PrairieHQ on their phone.
   - Tap **Clock In** at the start of their shift.
   - Select current activity: Yard / Shop Time, Travel / Transit, Job Site Work, or Lunch / Break.
2. **Crew Leader Group Clock-In**:
   - Foremen can clock in their entire crew simultaneously with a single tap.
   - Ideal for field crews traveling together in a single service truck.

---

## Geofencing & Location Verification

- When a technician clocks in or out, PrairieHQ captures device GPS coordinates.
- If clock-in occurs at a customer property, the punch is automatically linked to the active job for that property.
- Managers can review punch locations on a map to verify arrival at designated job sites.

---

## Timecard Auditing & Adjustments

Under **Resourcing → Clock In/Out**:
- Managers can review daily time entries across all crews.
- Missing punches (e.g., employee forgot to clock out at the end of the shift) are flagged with warnings.
- Supervisors can edit punch timestamps and add explanatory audit notes.
