# Payroll Management

The **Payroll** module aggregates verified employee timecards, calculates regular and overtime hours, and prepares clean summaries for payroll processing or accounting export.

## The Pay Period Workflow

1. Navigate to **Resourcing → Payroll**.
2. Select the **Pay Period** date range (e.g., Bi-weekly, Semi-monthly).
3. Review total hours:
   - **Regular Hours**: Standard hours worked up to daily/weekly overtime thresholds.
   - **Overtime (1.5x)**: Calculated according to company or regional overtime rules.
   - **Double Time (2.0x)**: Holiday or severe weather emergency shifts if applicable.
   - **Non-Billable Time**: Shop hours, travel time, and equipment maintenance.

---

## Finalizing & Exporting

1. Ensure all missing punches and timecard flags have been resolved.
2. Click **Lock Pay Period** to prevent further field edits.
3. Export payroll data via CSV or integration sync to your payroll provider (ADP, QuickBooks Payroll, Paychex, or Gusto).
