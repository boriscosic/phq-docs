# Approvals & Online Payments

Collecting rapid approvals on proposals and receiving timely payments are central to healthy cash flow.

## The Proposal Review & E-Signature Flow

1. Customer receives an email notification with a **Review Proposal** button.
2. Clicking the link opens the proposal in the secure PrairieHQ Client Portal.
3. The customer reviews:
   - Scope of work breakdown and deliverables.
   - Pricing schedule and payment terms.
   - Service frequency and legal disclaimers.
4. The client enters their full legal name and provides an e-signature on desktop (using a mouse) or mobile/tablet (using a touchscreen).
5. A PDF copy of the executed agreement is instantly emailed to all parties and stored on the customer account in PrairieHQ.

---

## Paying Invoices Online

PrairieHQ integrates directly with **Stripe** to process online customer payments:
- **Accepted Payment Methods**: Major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and bank debit / ACH transfers.
- **Card on File**: Customers can securely save payment credentials for recurring monthly maintenance billing.
- **Auto-Receipts**: Payment receipts are generated automatically upon successful charge and ledger records update in real time.
