# Client Portal Overview

The **PrairieHQ Client Portal** is a dedicated, self-service web experience for your commercial property managers, building owners, and residential clients.

## Client Benefits

Rather than relying on back-and-forth emails, property managers can access their dedicated portal 24/7 to:
- Review and digitally sign service estimates, contracts, and scope proposals.
- View and download current and historical invoices in PDF format.
- Pay balances securely online with instant payment confirmation.
- Keep track of active service agreements and warranty terms.

---

## Access & Security

- Customers access the portal via a branded URL (e.g., `https://portal.prairiehq.com`).
- Direct access links in proposal and invoice emails provide single-click authentication without complicated passwords.
- Role-based permissions within customer accounts allow company managers to specify who can sign contracts vs. who can only view billing.
