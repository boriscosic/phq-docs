# Navigation & Workspace

PrairieHQ is designed for high-density desktop and tablet workflows, allowing dispatchers, account managers, and crew leaders to navigate smoothly between operations and financials.

## The Global Sidebar

The left-hand sidebar is your primary navigation menu. You can collapse or expand it using the toggle icon at the bottom of the navigation drawer.

### Main Navigation Sections

- **Operations**:
  - **Work Schedule**: High-level dispatch board and route calendars.
  - **Crew List**: Real-time list of crews, current job assignments, and progress.
  - **On-Demand List**: Unscheduled or immediate service calls requiring quick dispatch.
  - **Work Calendar**: Full monthly/weekly calendar view of all upcoming visits.
  - **Job Tracking**: Map view with live location tracking and dispatch pins.

- **Workbook**:
  - **Customers**: Search and manage customer profiles, sites, and contacts.
  - **Documents**: Quotes, estimates, proposals, and customer sign-offs.
  - **Work Orders**: Master work orders, service intervals, and contracts.
  - **Site Reviews**: Quality inspections and grounds check sheets.

- **Financials**:
  - **Daily Financials**: Day-to-day revenue, target thermometers, and labor costs.
  - **Invoices**: Draft, sent, paid, and overdue invoices.
  - **P&Ls**: Profitability breakdowns by customer, service category, or division.
  - **Cash Flow**: Cash collections forecasting and receivables tracking.

- **Resourcing**:
  - **Employees**: Staff profiles, wage categories, and crew assignments.
  - **Clock In/Out**: Live timekeeping dashboard and punches.
  - **Time Off**: Vacation and absence requests.
  - **Payroll**: Exportable timesheets and hours summary.

---

## The Quick Action "+ New" Menu

Located at the top of the sidebar or header, the **+ New** button gives you one-click access to initiate common tasks without leaving your current screen:

- **+ Customer**: Open the drawer to register a new account.
- **+ Document**: Draft a new estimate or proposal.
- **+ Work Order**: Create a new operational work order.
- **+ Site Review**: Start a new inspection checklist.

---

## List Filtering & Search Standards

Every data table in PrairieHQ (Work Orders, Customers, Invoices, Employees) features standardized filtering:

1. **Global Search Input**: Search by title, customer name, work order number, or address.
2. **Column Filters**: Filter by status (e.g., `Draft`, `Scheduled`, `Completed`), date ranges, assigned crew, or tags.
3. **Saved Views**: Frequently used filter configurations can be bookmarked or saved for rapid access.
4. **Pagination & Page Size**: Adjust rows per page (25, 50, 100) or toggle dense view mode.
