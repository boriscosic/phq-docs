# Profit & Loss (P&L) Reports

The **P&L** reporting module gives finance managers and executives deep insight into operational profitability by job, customer account, service line, or branch division.

## Viewing P&L Reports

Under **Financials → P&Ls**:
- Filter by date range (Month to Date, Quarter, Year to Date, or Custom).
- Group reports by:
  - **Customer / Property**: See which commercial accounts yield the strongest margins.
  - **Division**: Commercial Maintenance, Landscape Construction, Irrigation, Snow & Ice.
  - **Crew / Vehicle**: Analyze vehicle route profitability and labor efficiency.

---

## Cost Allocations in PrairieHQ

P&L statements break down into:
1. **Gross Revenue**: Invoiced and accrued contract revenue.
2. **Direct Labor Costs**: Hourly technician wages, overtime premiums, and payroll taxes.
3. **Direct Material Costs**: Seed, mulch, fertilizer, de-icing salt, plants, and aggregate.
4. **Equipment & Fuel**: Fuel consumption and machine depreciation allocations.
5. **Gross Profit Margin (%)**: Direct dollar contribution and margin percentage.
