# Cash Flow Management

The **Cash Flow** tool gives leadership a forward-looking view of liquidity and receivables collections.

## Features

- **Receivables Timeline**: Predicts expected cash inflows based on invoice due dates and customer payment history.
- **Contract Billing Forecast**: Displays upcoming scheduled recurring invoice runs for the next 30, 60, and 90 days.
- **Aging Alerts**: Highlights critical overdue accounts requiring collections outreach before future service dispatches.
