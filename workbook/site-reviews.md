# Site Reviews & Inspections

**Site Reviews** (also known as Grounds Inspections, Quality Audits, or Check Sheets) enable account managers and field supervisors to inspect properties, ensure quality standards, and generate upsell opportunities.

## Why Use Site Reviews?

- **Quality Control**: Verify that mowing, edging, bed maintenance, or snow plowing meets contract standards.
- **Client Transparency**: Share branded inspection reports directly with property managers.
- **Upsell Identification**: Spot landscape hazards, dead shrubs, turf disease, or drainage issues and convert them directly into enhancement proposals.
- **Seasonal Hand-off**: Document pre-season and post-season property conditions before winter or spring services begin.

---

## Conducting a Site Review

1. Navigate to **Workbook → Site Reviews** and click **+ New Site Review**.
2. Select the **Customer** and **Property**.
3. Choose an **Audit Template** (e.g., "Monthly Grounds Quality Audit", "Winter Pre-Season Site Walk", "Irrigation System Check").
4. Complete the scored sections:
   - **Turf & Lawn Health**: Score 1–5, notes, and photos.
   - **Shrub & Tree Pruning**: Inspection notes and clearance verification.
   - **Flower Beds & Mulch**: Weed presence, mulch depth, edging quality.
   - **Hardscapes & Curbs**: Curb damage, litter presence, pavement condition.
5. Capture photos directly from your tablet or mobile camera.
6. Add general recommendations or immediate safety hazards.

---

## Generating Client Reports & Proposals

Once completed:
- Click **Export PDF Report** to generate a clean, branded inspection summary with photos and notes.
- Click **Create Enhancement Document** to immediately spin off a proposal with quoted line items for items identified during the walkthrough.
