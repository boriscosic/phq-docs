# Customer Management

The **Customers** module serves as the central hub for managing your clients, commercial accounts, property portfolios, and service locations.

## Customer Directory

Access the directory under **Workbook → Customers**. From here, you can:
- Filter accounts by status (`Active`, `Prospect`, `Inactive`).
- Search by customer name, account number, contact phone, or address.
- View lifetime value (LTV), open invoices, active work orders, and signed documents.

---

## Creating a Customer

1. Click the **+ New** button in the sidebar or top bar, or click **Add Customer** within the customer list.
2. Complete the customer details:
   - **Account Type**: Commercial Property, HOA, Retail Center, Industrial, or Residential.
   - **Company / Account Name**: The primary legal or business name.
   - **Billing Address**: Address for invoices and accounting sync.
   - **Default Payment Terms**: Net 15, Net 30, Due on Receipt, or Credit Card on File.
   - **Tax Profile**: Applicable state/provincial sales tax rules or exempt status.
3. Click **Save Customer**.

---

## Managing Properties & Service Locations

A single commercial customer may have multiple property sites (e.g., retail plazas, office campuses, or residential developments).

### Adding Properties
1. Open the customer details page and click the **Properties** tab.
2. Click **Add Property**.
3. Specify:
   - **Property Name / Identifier** (e.g., "North Hills Plaza - Bldg B").
   - **Physical Address & Coordinates** (used for field routing and geofenced clock-in).
   - **Site Access Notes** (gate codes, lockbox combinations, parking restrictions).
   - **Contact Persons** (property manager, on-site facility superintendent).

---

## Contact Directory

Under the **Contacts** tab of any customer, you can maintain multiple stakeholders:
- **Billing Contact**: Recipient of digital invoices and accounting statements.
- **Decision Maker**: Recipient for signing proposals and contract renewals.
- **Site Contact**: On-site point of contact for daily crew check-ins.
