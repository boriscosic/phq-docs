# Crew Tracking & Field View

**Crew Tracking** provides dispatchers and office staff with real-time operational visibility into crew progress, job statuses, and field locations.

## The Crew List View

Located under **Operations → Crew List**:
- Shows all active field crews for the selected day.
- Displays crew leader name, assigned technicians, and assigned vehicle/equipment.
- Real-time status indicators:
  - 🟢 **On Site**: Currently clocked into a job.
  - 🟡 **In Transit**: En route to next property.
  - ⚪ **Idle / On Break**: Clocked into lunch or yard time.
  - 🏁 **Route Finished**: All assigned jobs completed.

---

## Field Job Execution (Crew Leader Workflow)

When crew leaders arrive on site:
1. Open PrairieHQ on their mobile device or tablet.
2. Select the current job from their daily route list.
3. Tap **Start Job**: Records exact arrival timestamp and GPS location.
4. Review property notes, gate access codes, and service check items.
5. Record site photos (before/after) if required by the customer contract.
6. Tap **Complete Job**: Records exit timestamp, total person-hours consumed, and any field notes.

---

## Live GPS & Map Tracking

Under **Operations → Job Tracking**:
- Interactive map showing today's service locations and route progress.
- Color-coded pins indicate pending, in-progress, and completed stops.
- Allows dispatchers to answer client inquiries ("When will the crew arrive?") with accurate live data.
