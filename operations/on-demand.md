# On-Demand Jobs

While recurring contracts form the backbone of field service operations, commercial contractors frequently handle one-off, emergency, or ad-hoc requests—such as fallen tree removal, emergency irrigation repair, storm cleanup, or specialty mulch installations.

## What is an On-Demand Job?

An **On-Demand Job** is a single-occurrence work order designed for rapid dispatch without setting up complex recurring schedule rules.

---

## Creating an On-Demand Request

1. Navigate to **Operations → On-Demand List**.
2. Click **+ New On-Demand Job**.
3. Select the customer and property address.
4. Set the **Priority**:
   - `Emergency` (Immediate safety hazard or property impediment).
   - `High` (Same-day or next-day turnaround).
   - `Standard` (Within standard service window).
5. Specify scope details, target completion date, and estimated labor hours.
6. Assign an available crew or leave in the dispatch queue.

---

## Dispatching from the On-Demand Queue

- Dispatchers can review the unassigned On-Demand queue alongside today's active route map.
- Assign the job to the closest operating crew based on live GPS location and schedule capacity.
- The assigned crew immediately receives an update on their mobile route list.
