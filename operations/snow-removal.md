# Snow & Winter Operations

PrairieHQ includes specialized capabilities for winter service contractors managing commercial snow plowing, sidewalk clearing, de-icing, and anti-icing operations.

## Winter Operational Model

Snow removal operations differ fundamentally from summer maintenance:
- **Event-Driven**: Dispatches are triggered by snowfall depth, freezing rain, or storm forecasts rather than fixed weekly calendars.
- **Contract Tiers**:
  - **Per-Push / Per-Event**: Billed per clearing event based on accumulated depth brackets (e.g., 2–4 inches, 4–8 inches).
  - **Per-Application**: Salt, brine, or calcium chloride billed per pound, ton, or flat application rate.
  - **Seasonal Fixed**: Flat monthly fee covering unlimited events up to contract caps.
  - **Time & Materials**: Hourly equipment and operator rates.

---

## The Snow Removal Log

Access the log under **Workbook → Snow Removal Log**:
- Field operators record exact equipment utilized (truck plow, skid steer, loader, salter).
- Log weather conditions at arrival: temperature, snowfall accumulation, pavement condition (wet, packed snow, ice).
- Record materials dispersed (bags/pounds of salt or ice melt).
- Upload site condition photos (entryways cleared, parking bays plowed, handicap ramps salted).

---

## Liability & Slip-and-Fall Documentation

Accurate record-keeping is critical for risk mitigation:
- Every snow log entry captures immutable GPS coordinates and precise timestamps upon arrival and departure.
- Generates detailed, audit-ready site visit certificates to protect your company and property owners in the event of insurance claims or slip-and-fall litigation.
